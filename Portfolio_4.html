Digital circuits

LDR-Based Digital Counter

Counts objects by sensing when they block a beam of light.
About
A group project for the Digital Logic & Circuits Lab. It counts objects that block a beam of light and shows the count on a 7-segment display.
My role
Project coordinator in a team of five
Tools
LDR, NE555 timer (monostable mode), CD4026 decade counter IC, 7-segment display, resistors, capacitor, power supply
Outcome
The circuit counted objects correctly from 0 to 9, adding one for each interruption of the light, and worked reliably under proper lighting. Known limits: ambient light, single-digit count, careful alignment needed.
Power

Transformer Safety Device

Two smaller transformers share the load, so one big transformer is not overloaded.
Problem
A single large transformer can overload and get damaged at full load, and replacing it is costly and slow.
Approach
Use two smaller transformers instead of one large one, so the load is shared evenly and each is under less stress.
My role
Project coordinator, builder and organizer
Outcome
Stress and heat on the transformers dropped by about 30%, and overall efficiency increased.
Antenna simulation

AESA Radar Antenna

A 10 GHz antenna array that steers its radar beam electronically, designed and simulated.
About
An AESA radar steers its beam electronically with many small antenna elements and no moving parts. For my Electromagnetic Fields and Waves course, I designed an X-band patch antenna for 10 GHz and extended it to an 8-element array in simulation. No physical prototype was built.
My role
Individual project: calculated the antenna dimensions from first principles, ran the simulations and wrote the report
Tools
COMSOL Multiphysics 6.3 (Electromagnetic Waves, Frequency Domain, and the Array Factor operator), Rogers RT/duroid 5880 substrate model, microstrip transmission-line equations
Outcome
Return loss of −42 dB at 10.00 GHz with a 380 MHz bandwidth (9.80 to 10.18 GHz). The single patch had a directivity of 8.49 dB. In the 8×1 array simulation, the peak gain reached 17.6 dBi and the beam steered up to 60° off-axis without grating lobes.
